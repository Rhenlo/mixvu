# Mixvu support

Open an issue: https://github.com/Rhenlo/mixvu/issues

Include platform (Windows / Android / iOS), camera brand, ONVIF or Ring/Blink, and whether the phone and camera share Wi-Fi.

## We will not

Help anyone view a camera they do not own or do not have written permission to view.

## Macros

- ONVIF: same Wi-Fi, admin user, port 80 or 8000, then paste RTSP.
- Ring / Blink: they are cloud. Sign in. They will never be `rtsp://` on the LAN.
- The web preview cannot scan a router. That is not a defect.
- Refunds: Apple, Google, or Microsoft. We do not hold the card.
