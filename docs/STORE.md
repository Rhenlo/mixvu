## Short

Stop bouncing between the Ring app, a Chinese IP-cam app, and the laptop webcam. Mixvu is one desk. ONVIF and RTSP plug in on your Wi-Fi. Ring and Blink sign in. This device’s camera is a tile too.

Never say “works with all cameras.”
