# Mixvu

**Every camera. One desk.**

Mixvu is a mixed-brand camera desk. ONVIF and RTSP plug in on your Wi-Fi. Ring and Blink sign in. This phone’s camera is a tile too.

It is **not** “every camera on earth.” Wyze official RTSP was pulled. Nest is planned. You may only add cameras you own or have written permission to view.

## Status

Closed beta. Not on the App Store or Play yet. 50 TestFlight / Play Internal seats.

This repo is the public listing and the owner checklist. The running desk is the Mixvu app. A browser tab **cannot** scan your LAN. The installed bridge on a PC on the same Wi-Fi can.
